Bad Bus Dashcam

Records video with time, GPS position, speed and bus details burned into every frame, and builds a report you can send to the transport authority.

Camera, microphone and location permission are needed. Nothing is uploaded: everything stays on your phone until you save or share it.

What are you photographing?

The photo is taken from the live camera with the time, GPS position and bus details stamped on it. Hold steady and get close.

Bus details

Shown on the video overlay and in the report. Fill in what you can — you can edit later.

Evidence (this session)

Save or share files before closing the app — they are kept in memory only.

Complaint report

Copy this into the Ministry of Transport public transport complaint form (gov.il; info line *8787) or the operator's complaint form. Complaints must be filed within 2 months; anonymous complaints are not handled. Attach the video and photos. For traffic offences you can also go to the police.
